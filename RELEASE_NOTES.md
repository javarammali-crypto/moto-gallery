Download the APK below and open it on your phone to install, or update from inside the app.

- New albums: tap New album in Albums, or select photos and tap Album to copy or move them
- Hide albums you don't want to see (long-press an album). Music cover art is hidden automatically
- Camera only filter for the Photos page, and it stays the way you leave it
- The month and year stay at the top while you scroll
- Swipe up on a photo to see its details
- Long-press text in a photo to select and copy it (or tap the text button)
- Long-press a person, pet or object to lift it off the background: copy, save, share or make a sticker
- Editor: new Exposure slider; filter and background rows move along as you tap
